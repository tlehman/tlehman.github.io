Rubik's Cube Scanner & Solver

Scan your scrambled cube with the camera, fix any mis-read stickers on the unfolded map, then get a full solution. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

1 Scan the six faces

Press “Start camera”, then hold a face inside the grid.
Hold the cube so one face fills the grid. Keep the top of the cube pointing up. Capture the faces in order; rotate the cube as prompted between shots.

2 Check the colours

Tap a sticker to paint it with the selected colour. Centres are fixed — they define each face.
